This mod adds a handful of spells used to perform special close-range attacks.
When using these spells, an indicator will appear when you are close enough to an enemy to attack:
[stab indicator in game image/gif]

Stab! adds [b]8[/b] spells, which can each be toggled on/off in the mod's settings.

Some of the spells drop from specific bosses, and the others can be obtained from shadow bosses in parallel worlds.
If Foolish Flame is enabled, the spells can also be acquired from hell bounties.

Cross-mod interactions:
[b][url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3795285917]Foolish Flame[/url][/b] A new heat gauge, and the 'Stabbing Fire' spell inflicts magic fire on Stab!
[b][url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3313573016]Souls[/url][/b] The 'Essence Extractor' spell grants souls on Stab!
