[stab title]

This mod adds a handful of spells used to perform special close-range attacks.
When using these spells, an indicator will appear when you are close enough to an enemy to attack:
[stab indicator in game image]

Stab! adds [b]7[/b] spells, which can each be toggled on/off in the mod's settings:
[big earner]
[stabbing fire]
[misericorde]
[getaway]
[essence extractor]
[stabage]
[stab power]

Cross-mod interactions:
[b][url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3795285917]Foolish Flame[/url][/b] 'Stabbing Fire' spell inflicts magic fire
[b][url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3313573016]Souls[/url][/b] 'Essence Extractor' spell grants souls
